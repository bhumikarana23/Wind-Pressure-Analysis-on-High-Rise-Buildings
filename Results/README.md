# Results & Analysis

## Overview

This folder is dedicated to documenting and analysing the outputs obtained from the ANSYS CFD simulations conducted as part of the project **Wind Pressure Analysis on High-Rise Buildings Under Extreme Wind Conditions**.

It will serve as the central location for numerical simulation visualisations, observations, result interpretation and subsequent comparison with experimental findings.

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Pressure Distribution

- Examine pressure variations across different building surfaces.
- Study the distribution of wind-induced pressure around the building geometry.
- Identify areas exhibiting variations in simulated pressure.

### 2. Velocity Distribution

- Examine airflow behaviour around the high-rise building.
- Study velocity variations in the computational domain.
- Interpret the flow patterns obtained from CFD simulations.

### 3. Numerical Result Interpretation

- Review the generated simulation outputs.
- Document relevant observations from pressure and velocity contours.
- Relate numerical findings to the objectives of the project.

### 4. Experimental Comparison – Upcoming

- Compare numerical simulation findings with experimental observations.
- Examine similarities and differences between computational and experimental results.
- Document the findings following experimental validation.
