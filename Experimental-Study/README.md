# Experimental Investigation & Validation

## Overview

This folder is reserved for the experimental investigation phase of the academic project **Wind Pressure Analysis on High-Rise Buildings Under Extreme Wind Conditions**.

The planned experimental study aims to investigate wind behaviour around the building model and establish a basis for comparison with the numerical results obtained through ANSYS CFD simulations.

## Planned Work

- Prepare the physical building model for experimental investigation.
- Establish suitable experimental conditions for studying wind flow behaviour.
- Observe airflow patterns around the building model.
- Record relevant experimental observations and measurements.
- Compare experimental findings with numerical simulation outputs.

## Purpose of Experimental Validation

Experimental investigation is intended to provide an additional basis for evaluating the numerical simulation results.

The comparison will help examine the consistency between computational predictions and experimental observations.
